    sources & credits

    This tool displays chord symbols only — no melodies, no lyrics, no recordings. Song titles and composer names are shown as factual metadata. Chord changes follow common lead-sheet practice, cross-checked against jazz-education references.

    The soloist's phrasing figures are measured from the Weimar Jazz Database — 456 hand-transcribed solos from the Jazzomat Research Project, Hochschule für Musik Franz Liszt Weimar, under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0). No transcription is reproduced; what ships is derived statistics.

    Samples: MusyngKite soundfont kit (CC BY-SA 3.0), the Splendid Grand Piano, and the vibraphone from the Versilian Community Sample Library (CC0), via smplr (MIT) · scheduling by Tone.js (MIT) · drums synthesized in-browser. Changes for most tunes come from the iRealPro Corpus of Jazz Standards (CC BY 4.0). This site is open source under GPLv3. For personal practice & education.
