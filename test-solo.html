solo lab

Generates an Inspire-mode solo, shows every note colored by its harmonic function, and measures the line against reference values from jazz-corpus research. Use it to hear a change and see whether it actually moved the numbers.

Press space to play/stop, g to generate a new solo. With play exactly the line shown ticked, the notes below are the notes you hear, repeated every chorus — so you can read along and find out what a metric actually sounds like. Untick it to let the generator improvise a fresh line each chorus the way the app does; the annotated view is then a sample, not a transcript.
